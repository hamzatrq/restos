# v0 — what one pilot restaurant needs to run a real service day

**Rule for this document: if a pilot can open tomorrow without it, it is not v0.**
Everything else is v1. This file is short on purpose and stays short.

## Already works (measured 2026-08-23, demonstrated end to end twice)

Owner authors a priced menu in the back office → publishes → a real Electron till
rings it at the price typed → sends to a durable kitchen spooler → settles by
method → closes the shift → owner reads the day. Staff identify, unlock against
Argon2id, and every write is authorized server-side.

Do not rebuild any of it. `plans/wave-1/running-the-stack.md` is how you start it.

### Gap 3's residue — the per-key device migrations, named rather than left to a grep

`01-F87` made these possible and performs none of them. Each is a separate, surgical act:

- **`paid_out.approval_threshold_paisa` and `discount.approval_threshold_bps`** — `authorize.ts`'s
  own comments call both constants *"a placeholder for a configuration surface that does not exist,
  and the FIRST thing to delete when it does"*. The surface exists now. The design question in
  front of the migration: `authorizeWrites` takes both as VALUES captured at construction, so
  reading the store there would freeze the threshold at boot — the drift `tax-posture.ts`'s
  *"no memo and no construction-time capture"* rule refuses, and `01-F87`'s point-in-time read is
  at the ACT. The migration is therefore a thunk on `AuthorizedWritesDeps`, which moves
  `authorize.ts` and ~10 oracle files. ⚠ **`05-F33` already ruled the default to ZERO**, so the
  migration also changes behaviour: an unconfigured org escalates every paid-out, which R65 accepts
  by name.
- **`tax.posture_matrix` and `charge.rounding_paisa`** — `16-F33` (a) requires the five readers of
  `01-F82`'s `billed_total` to resolve the posture and the step **together or not at all**, so this
  is one change across `settlement-guard`, `settlement-closer`, `line-advance`,
  `aggregator-settlement` and `printing`, plus `gateway.ts`. It belongs with gap 2's owner.
- **`commission.by_provider` has no device migration and never will** — `02 §Layer 2` keeps it off
  the till, and `configPage` withholds it. Its reader is `14-F24`'s channel-economics report.
- **`taxCellForTender` carries an `@unreached-owed` marker**: `16-F32` (R58) puts the tender-channel
  choice before the unpaid bill prints and no surface offers it, so nothing can select a non-default
  cell yet. The caller lands with that surface.

**only** where a defect is permanent and unfixable by a later release: money

get one review each — that is the only gate v0 keeps.

## Open money defects — found by adversarial review of `8dee4bd`, not yet fixed

Both reproduced against shipping code. Neither is visible at the shipped default
(posture `none`, step 100, whole-rupee prices); both are live at configurations
the corpus explicitly permits. **Fix before a pilot is given a rate or a step.**

**1. `01-F33`'s `uncovered_addition` ceiling is wrong in BOTH directions.**
`settlement-closer.ts:160` attests a **rounded, tax-inclusive** `billed_paisa`;
`merge.ts:1378` compares it against `billedEffective`, the **raw, tax-blind,
unrounded** line sum. Post-`01-F82`/`02-F63` those are different quantities and
the check straddles them.

- *False positive*: at step 1000, posture `none`, a single Rs 404 line settled
  correctly and closed with nothing added — `["uncovered_addition"]`. It means
  *a line was added after the close*, and it is now stamped on roughly half of
  correctly settled orders at any step > 1.
- *False negative*: at `exclusive` 1600 bps, a genuine **Rs 60 line added after
  the settlement act** → `[]`. Any post-close addition up to the tax amount is
  invisible. **This half predates R70** and is live at any exclusive posture
  with no rounding at all.

Nothing sees either: `exceptions_json` has exactly one reader
(`auditor.ts:349`, a `money_overflow` regex). The fix belongs at the ceiling's
definition — a **doc 01 spec act on `01-F33`**, not an edit.

**2. A sub-rupee step strands the cashier — at a step `02-F63` (c) blesses by name.**
At `charge_rounding_paisa = 1` under `exclusive`, both ends of the human path
truncate: `MoneyValue.tsx:67` renders through `rupeesFromPaisa`, and
`TenderPanel.tsx:128` accepts whole rupees only. Measured on a Rs 404 line at
1600 bps: due `46_864`, glass shows **`Rs 468`**, keying 468 gives `46_800`,
`alreadySettled` → **null**, and the Pay surface then reads `DUE Rs 0` for ever.

That is the same failure `8dee4bd` removed, surviving at a blessed config.
`tax-on-the-bill.test.ts` §E *does* iterate `step: "1"` — but it compares
against the raw projected number, never against what the glass shows or the pad
can produce, so it passes while the human path fails. **A guard built correctly
and aimed one case away.**

Minimum fix: refuse a granularity that is not a whole number of rupees at
`resolveChargeRoundingPaisa`, until the pad and `27-F23` can express paisa — or
state the restriction in `02-F63` (c), whose text currently says the opposite.

**3. `02-F63` (g) is factually wrong about a money term.** It names *"an
all-comped order"* as a zero-total case; `line-void.ts:147` records that comp is
**recorded and not subtracted**. The FR authored by that change misstates it.

**4. The zero-billed sentinel's second door is wider than reported.** A
**zero-priced line** reaches it with no void, no rounding and no act at all —
`01-F60` permits price 0 precisely so *free* is distinguishable from
*forgotten*. Same doc-01 fix; larger blast radius than the fully-voided framing.

Smaller: `tax-posture.ts:175` names `roundPaisaToGranularity`, deleted by the
rename; `sync-client/src/index.ts:63` justifies an export as *"host apps must
render the order total"*, false as of `8dee4bd`, whose only remaining consumer
is `packages/auditor`; and `tax-on-the-bill.test.ts` §E's first case asserts the
**rounded** charge under a message calling it *"the unrounded subtotal"*.
